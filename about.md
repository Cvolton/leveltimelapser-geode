# Level Timelapser
This mod adds a button to the info pages of both created and online levels, which allows you to see how they were likely built.

## Usage:
1. Click the button with the Creator Point icon ![.](frame:GJ_hammerIcon_001.png?scale=0.5) on the left-hand side of any level info page.
2. Choose your desired timelapse speed.
3. Enjoy the show and look around the editor to see what's currently being placed.

---

## How does this work?
Starting with 2.2, every level saves its objects in the exact order they were placed. This mod simulates placing these objects again into a blank level.

For levels made in 2.1 and older, the placement order will be inaccurate, as objects were split into groups based on their X position. However, the mod will still work just fine; the order will just differ from the one used during the creation of the level.
